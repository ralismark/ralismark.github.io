After a dear friend taught me how to patch my shorts, I've gotten into a bit of a sewing phase!
I'm currently nearing completion on a [Coquelicot](https://www.wildflowerdesignpatterns.com/product-page/coquelicot-skirt)-inspired split-side skirt, but before that I was making some really cute heat packs!

They are filled with raw red bean[^filling], meaning you can microwave them for a minute or two and they stay warm for quite a white.

They aren't based on an existing pattern, instead I drafted them myself!
Here's the digitised version -- print on A4 with no margins, the red border should exactly match the paper edge.

Making patterns myself is pretty fun!
Shelley was inspired by floor turtles I saw on Pinterest, and fundamentally they are the same pattern with slightly different shapes.
But my brain is the kind that likes creating the pattern from scratch and winging it :)

[^filling]: I've seen people online use a lot of different fillings:
	Rice was the most common (but you have to be careful of it burning), and cherry pits surprised me.
	My family has always used red bean, to great success, but I didn't see _a single_ mention of it being a viable filling option for some reason...
