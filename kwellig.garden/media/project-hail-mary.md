It's a fun book, it's a pretty easy read -- I binged the entire 500 pages of my copy in about 4 days.
I'm a bit of a immense nerd, and I really enjoyed all the science and engineering that Grace does -- he spends a good few chapters just deducing that he's in space and not orbiting the sun!
It feels akin to watching a Nilered video.

I really wanna give credit for the design of the Eridians, and all the biology in the book.
Eridian biology and society are so unique and truly _alien_ in such a well-founded way that everything feels so plausible.
And any commonalities between them and humans are similarly natural and justified.

Now, I watched the movie first, then heard about all the details that didn't make it to movie, then watched the movie again, before _finally_ reading the book.
And the book is so much lighter on the emotional beats, barely any weight is given to the pivotal moments in the plot.
Even when things are dire the prose is still pretty silly and corny -- I've heard it described as "insufferably reddit".
The character arc of Grace learning to be brave, by having someone to be brave for (is that a movie-only quote?), is only barely there... and I really wish Grace would forgive Stratt.
And the characters too -- the movies make Stratt a much more complex character, Rocky and Grace bond more to really show their bond.

Another difference between the book and the movie is Rocky saving Grace.
While in the movie Rocky simply drags Grace to the medbay, in the book Rocky is only able to free Grace from being trapped under the seat, then Grace drags Rocky up the ship back to his side.
I like the book version more -- Grace caring so much about Rocky.
I also like the movie spacecraft more, though the book really conveys how banged up the Hail Mary gets and how much Rocky repairs it.

HOWEVER.
THE MOVIE DID NOT SHOW ANTARCTICA BEING NUKED.
Those scenes of the climatologist Dr. Leclerc's guilt at doing all this were some of most impactful in the whole book, and it would've been such a good point of comparison for Grace.

Finally, the things the book makes me wish for more of.
I wish there was more of the characters... though maybe written by someone else.
I wish there was more about the prime crew (and an "everyone lives" fix-it fic), I would've loved to see more of Ilyukhina I love her personality sooo freaking much that I made a TTRPG character inspired by her personality.
I've also been reading [Equanimity](https://archiveofourown.org/works/84003576), a strattland timeloop fic that is so well written the later chapters made me bawl for a solid hour.

---

One more thing.
The book claims that it takes more fuel to reach Venus than Mars.
Coincidentally, I happened to have the [Solar System Delta-V Map](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Solar_system_delta_v_map.svg) printed and laid out on the wall of my bedroom.
When I got to that claim, I SPRINTED to my bedroom to check the chart.
And turns out, it's actually easier to get to Mars than Venus -- it's 839 m/s to Venus capture, compared to 1061 m/s to Mars capture!
