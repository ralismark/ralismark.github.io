DNF around a third of the way in, then I skimmed through the last few chapters.

The prose is definitely solid, and the unfolding premise of the story is great!
It's a meditation on accepting that things won't ever go back to normal.
But it was too meandering and undirected, and for that it wasn't interesting enough to keep me engaged the entire way.
And personally, it didn't hit that hard emotionally.

Not my cup of tea, unfortunately.
